Understanding the Science
What is Laser Hair Reduction?

Laser hair reduction uses focused light energy to target and destroy hair follicles, preventing future hair growth. The treatment works by targeting hair during specific phases of its natural growth cycle.

01

Anagen — Active Growth Phase

Hair follicles are actively growing and highly receptive to laser treatment. This is the optimal window for effective hair reduction.

02

Catagen — Transition Phase

Hair stops growing and the follicle begins to shrink. During this phase, hair is less responsive to laser energy.

03

Telogen — Resting & Shedding Phase

Hair is no longer growing and is preparing to shed. Hair in this phase is not affected by the laser treatment.

Key Insight: The laser specifically targets hair in the Anagen phase, which is why multiple sessions are essential — different hairs enter the active phase at different times. Treated hairs shed over several weeks, and each session progressively reduces more active hair for lasting results.

Your Journey
What to Expect

After the First Treatment

Immediate Aftercare: Hair may appear to grow back normally initially — this is completely expected.

In 7–10 Days: Hair from the active growth phase (Anagen) begins to shed, leaving the treated area progressively smoother over 2–3 weeks.

Mild Redness: Temporary redness or warmth is normal and subsides within a few hours. Follow post-treatment instructions carefully.

Between Treatment Sessions

Targeting Active Follicles: Each session targets more hair in its active growth phase, permanently reducing hair over time.

Slower Regrowth: Hair becomes softer, finer, and less noticeable with each successive session.

Smoother Skin: Treated areas feel progressively smoother as hair regrowth decreases significantly.

Reduced Ingrown Hairs: Over time, ingrown hairs diminish, leaving skin more even-toned and irritation-free.

Post-Treatment Completion

Long-Term Reduction: Expect up to 80% hair reduction within 6–8 sessions. Keratosis pilaris also improves.

Minimal Maintenance: Enjoy smooth, confident skin with only occasional maintenance sessions required.

Treatment Plan
Sessions & Disclaimers

6–8 Sessions

Recommended for optimal results. Multiple sessions ensure most hair follicles are treated during their active growth phase. The exact number varies by hair and skin type and is personalised to your needs.

4–6 Weeks Gap

Between sessions, allowing new hair to enter the growth phase for maximum treatment effectiveness. Facial hair may need shorter intervals, while legs may require longer gaps.

Important Disclaimers

Results Vary

Outcomes depend on individual hair and skin type; consultation provides personalised insights.

PCOS & Hormonal Conditions

Hormonal imbalances may require up to twice as many sessions. Men may also need additional sessions.

Temporary Shedding

Hair shedding after treatment is normal and expected over several weeks.

Gradual Process

Results develop over multiple sessions. Most see 80% hair reduction in 6–8 sessions.

Follow Guidelines

Pre and post-treatment care is essential for best results and to avoid side effects.

Maintenance Sessions

Periodic maintenance may be needed to sustain smooth, hair-free skin long-term.
