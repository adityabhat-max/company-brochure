The Process
How Does Botox Work?

Botox works by blocking the release of acetylcholine, a chemical messenger that signals muscles to contract. By interrupting this signal at targeted injection sites, the muscles relax, and the overlying skin smoothens out â giving you a naturally refreshed look.
